A small writing ritual.

Let the next word come.

Write without overthinking. Pause, and your last words begin to disappear. Type again to stop them. Choose a mode that fits what you want to keep; the clock starts with your first word.

In Journal and Let it go, set two independent timers: Write for controls your session length (1–240 minutes). Delete after controls how long you can stop typing (1 second–10 minutes). Changing modes never changes your chosen delay. Keep typing to stop words disappearing.

Four ways to write.

Journal is a timed page saved locally, with recovery snapshots before automatic deletion. Let it go is disposable: it saves nothing and offers no recovery. 750 Words is untimed, saved locally, and has erasing off by default. Brainstorm is an untimed local page for questions and answers, with no automatic erasing.

750 Words. A page for yourself.

Inspired by the freewriting spirit of The Artist’s Way. Aim for 750 words, without editing yourself as you go. This is a digital word-goal practice, not the book’s original three handwritten morning pages.

There is no timer in 750 Words. Automatic deletion is off by default; you can turn on erasing after a pause. Your draft stays in this browser. Reach the goal, then finish or keep writing. You can also finish early and keep what you wrote. Words are counted as groups separated by spaces or line breaks.

Let it go. Then let it go.

Let it go is disposable. It has no autosave, copy/export action, or recovery snapshots. Everything remaining is erased when the session ends, you end it early, or you leave this page. Its session deadline continues while you switch tabs. This is not secure erasure or protection against screenshots or browser/OS memory.

Your browser. Not our servers.

Your writing is not sent to us. We do not receive or store a copy. Journal and 750 Words save drafts only in this browser, with no account or cloud sync. Brainstorm saves locally in this app. Let it go saves nothing.

A local way back in Journal.

Recovery keeps exact snapshots from before automatic deletions—not a stitched-together transcript. Your browser keeps the original before the first deletion, plus up to 19 recent snapshots. Your manual edits and removed history aren’t reconstructed.

In Journal, switching tabs safely stops the clock until you resume. Reloaded drafts also wait for you. Clearing browser data can remove your drafts and recovery copies. They do not sync to another browser or device, and we cannot restore them for you. Download writing you want to keep. This app does not encrypt local storage.
