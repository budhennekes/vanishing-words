Choose your time. Begin with a thought, not a plan. The clock starts with your first word.

In Journal and Rant, set two independent timers: Write for controls your session length (1–240 minutes). Delete after controls how long you can stop typing (1 second–10 minutes). Changing modes never changes your chosen delay. Keep typing to stop words disappearing.

750 Words. A page for yourself.

Inspired by the freewriting spirit of The Artist’s Way. Aim for 750 words, without editing yourself as you go. This is a digital word-goal practice, not the book’s original three handwritten morning pages.

There is no timer or automatic deletion in 750 Words. Your draft stays in this browser. Reach the goal, then finish or keep writing. You can also finish early and keep what you wrote. Words are counted as groups separated by spaces or line breaks.

Rant. Then let it go.

Rant is disposable. It has no autosave, copy/export action, or recovery snapshots. Everything remaining is erased when the session ends, you end it early, or you leave this page. Its session deadline continues while you switch tabs. This is not secure erasure or protection against screenshots or browser/OS memory.

A way back in Journal.

Recovery keeps exact snapshots from before automatic deletions—not a stitched-together transcript. We keep the original before the first deletion, plus up to 19 recent snapshots. Your manual edits and removed history aren’t reconstructed.

In Journal, switching tabs safely stops the clock until you resume. Reloaded drafts also wait for you. Everything stays in this browser’s local storage; nothing is sent to a server. Export important writing.
